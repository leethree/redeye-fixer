Red-eye Fixer

Open a photo or drop it here, then click or tap each red eye.

Your photo stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded.

Opens
JPEG and PNG. WebP, GIF and BMP work in most browsers. HEIC (iPhone) only in Safari.
Saves
JPEG photos as JPEG (quality 97) with their camera and scanner details kept. Other formats as PNG, without those details.
Limits
You mark each eye yourself; there is no automatic detection. Made for red and orange eyes in people, not the yellow or green eye-shine of pets. Colours are saved as standard sRGB, so wide-colour photos from recent phones lose a little vividness. Very large photos may not open on phones. Marks are not kept if you close or reload the page.
Eye
Use "iris" when the skin is as warm as the red glow.
Click an eye to fix it. Drag a mark to move it. Drag elsewhere to pan, scroll to zoom. Right-click or Delete removes a mark; [ and ] resize; Space compares; Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes. Tap an eye to fix it. Drag a mark to move it. Drag elsewhere to pan, pinch to zoom.
